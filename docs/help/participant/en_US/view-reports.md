# View your reports

Reports are ready only after the PT provider **finalizes** the
shipment. Before that, the list is empty for that shipment.

## Individual report

Your lab's own result and score for one shipment.

1. Open **View PT Result → Individual Report**.
2. To narrow the list, pick a **Shipment Date** range or a
   **Scheme**. Then click **Get Report**.
3. Find your shipment by its **Shipment Code**.
4. Click the link in the **Report** column to download the PDF.

To download several reports at once, tick them and click
**Download Selected Reports**.

## Summary report

The overall results of one shipment across all labs.

1. Open **View PT Result → Summary Report**.
2. Pick a **Shipment Date** range and a **Scheme**. Then click
   **Get Report**.
3. Click the link in the **Report** column.

## Common questions

- **My report is not on the list.** The shipment is not
  finalized yet. Contact your PT provider if the result due
  date passed long ago.
- **The result says Unsatisfactory.** Read the report for the
  samples that did not match. Then see **Corrective Actions**.
