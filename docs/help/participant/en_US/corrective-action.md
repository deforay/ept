# Corrective actions

A corrective action tells your lab what to fix after a result
that did not match.

## See the corrective actions for a shipment

1. Open **Corrective Actions**.
2. Pick a **Shipment Date** range and a **Scheme**. Then click
   **Get Report**.
3. Find the shipment by its **Shipment Code**. The **Corrective
   Action** column shows what to do.

## CAPA

**CAPA** (Corrective and Preventive Action) appears in the menu
only if your PT provider turned it on. It lists your shipments
with their **Final Result**. Click **Action** on a shipment row
to record what your lab did to fix the problem.

To save the list as a file, click **Export Report**.
