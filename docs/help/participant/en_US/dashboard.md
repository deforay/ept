# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after you log in.

## What's on the page

- **What do you want to do today?** — four quick links:
  **Shipment Response**, **Individual Report**, **Summary
  Reports** and **Edit Profile**.
- **Shipments waiting for your results** — every shipment that
  still needs your results, with its due date. Shipments marked
  **Due soon** close first. Click **Enter results** to open the
  result form. If a shipment says **Finish your draft**, you
  started it and saved a draft.
- **Your recent results** — your latest score for each scheme.
  Results appear here only after the PT provider finalizes the
  shipment.

If the waiting list says *You are all caught up*, there is
nothing for you to do right now.

## Common questions

- **A shipment I expected is not on the list.** The PT provider
  has not shipped it yet, or your lab is not on that shipment.
  Contact your PT provider.
- **My score is missing.** Scores appear after the shipment is
  finalized. Until then, the PT provider is still checking the
  results.
