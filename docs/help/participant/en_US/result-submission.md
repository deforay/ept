# Submit PT results

This page lists the shipments for your lab. You enter your
results here.

## Enter your results

1. Find the shipment by its **Shipment** code. Check the
   **Participant** name on the same row.
2. Check the **Result Due Date**. After that date you cannot
   change the response.
3. Click **Enter Response**.
4. Fill in the form. Enter the dates, the test kits you used,
   and the result for each sample.
5. Click **Submit**.
6. If a review page opens, check your answers. Then click the
   green **Confirm Response** button at the bottom.

Your response is **not saved** until you click **Confirm
Response**. If the row still shows **Enter Response**, the
response was not saved. Open it again and finish.

## The buttons on each row

| Button | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Enter Response** | You have not sent results for this shipment yet |
| **View/Edit** | You sent results. Open them to check or change them before the due date |
| **View/Edit Draft** | You saved a draft (TB form only). Open it and finish |
| **Delete Response** | Removes your response so you can start again |

## If you could not test the panel

Open the result form and tick **PT panel not tested**. Then
give the reason. Do not enter negative results for samples you
did not test.

## Other lists in the menu

- **All Schemes** — every shipment for your lab, including old
  ones.
- **Defaulted Schemes** — shipments whose due date has passed.

## Common questions

- **The shipment is not on the list.** The PT provider has not
  shipped it yet, or your lab is not on that shipment. Contact
  your PT provider.
- **I cannot change my response.** The due date has passed, or
  the shipment is finalized. Contact your PT provider.
