# My account

The **Manage** menu holds your account pages.

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **My Login Information** | Your name and contact details |
| **PT Participant Info** | The labs your login enters results for |
| **Scheme Information** | The schemes each of your labs takes part in |
| **Change Primary Email** | Change the email you log in with |
| **Change Password** | Set a new password |

## Change your password

1. Open **Manage → Change Password**.
2. Confirm the lab shown is yours. Click **Yes, continue**. If it
   is not, click **Not me, sign out**.
3. Enter your **Old Password**.
4. Enter the **New Password** twice. You cannot reuse an old
   password.

## Change your login email

1. Open **Manage → Change Primary Email**.
2. Enter your current email, then the new email twice.
3. Click **Change mail address**.
4. Open the link that ePT sends to the new email. The change
   takes effect only after you open the link.

## Common questions

- **A lab is missing from PT Participant Info.** Your login is
  not linked to that lab. Ask your PT provider to link it.
- **I forgot my password.** Log out, then click **Forgot
  Password?** on the login page.
