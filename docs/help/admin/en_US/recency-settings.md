# HIV Recency (RTRI) Settings

This page sets how Rapid Test for Recent Infection (RTRI)
shipments are scored.

## Scoring

- **Minimum Passing Score**: the total score a participant needs
  to pass.
- **Panel/Shipment Score** and **Documentation Score**: how the
  total score splits between the panel results and the
  documentation. The two parts make up the total.

## Testing options

**No. of Days for Sample Rehydration** sets how long participants
wait between rehydrating and testing. Choose **Allow Same Day
Testing**, **Testing after 1 day only** or **Testing after 2 days
only**.

## Report metadata

**Report Version** and **Report Effective Date** print in the
footer of every report PDF.

Click **Update** to save. Changes apply the next time a shipment
is evaluated.
