# Participant Trends

**Reports → Participant Trends** shows how participants respond and perform across the shipments of one scheme. The page heading is **Participant Performance Overview**.

## Filters

Click **Expand Filters** to show the filters. Set them, then click **Get Report**.

| Filter | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Date range | Includes shipments whose shipment date falls in this range. | Last 180 days |
| Scheme | The scheme to report on. Required. | None. If only one scheme is active, that scheme. |
| Shipment | Limits the report to one shipment. | All shipments in the date range |

When only one scheme is active, the report runs as soon as the page opens.

Each active filter shows as a chip next to the **Expand Filters** button.

- Click a chip to open the filter panel on that filter.
- Click **×** on a chip to remove that filter.

## Summary tiles

The four tiles add up every shipment in the report.

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Enrolled | The number of participants enrolled, and the number of shipments. |
| Response rate | The percentage of enrolled participants who responded. |
| On time | The percentage of responses submitted by the due date. |
| Pass rate | The percentage of evaluated responses that passed. Shows **Not evaluated yet** before evaluation. |

## Trend by shipment

The chart shows the response rate, the on-time rate and the pass rate of each shipment, in shipment date order. Point at a shipment to see its three values.

The chart only shows when the report has two or more shipments.

## Shipments table

Each row is one shipment.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Shipment | The shipment code and the shipment date. |
| Enrolled | The number of participants enrolled. |
| Responded | The number who responded, and the response rate. |
| On time | The percentage of responses submitted by the due date. |
| Pass rate | The percentage of evaluated responses that passed, with a bar. |
| Ratings | A bar split into the three ratings below. Point at the bar to see the numbers. |

A shipment that is not evaluated yet shows **Not evaluated yet** in place of the pass rate and the ratings.

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Excellent | Passed with a full score. |
| Satisfactory | Passed below a full score. |
| Unsatisfactory | Failed. |

Click a shipment to show its regions under the row. Each region shows the same columns as the shipment. Participants without a region are grouped under **No region**, at the end. Click the shipment again to hide its regions.

## How participants are counted

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Enrolled | Every participant enrolled in the shipment. |
| Responded | Submitted results, including late results, or reported that they could not test. |
| On time | Responded, and the response was not late. |
| Evaluated | Passed or failed. Participants who did not respond are never counted as failed. |

## Download Excel

**Download Excel** saves the report as an Excel file with two sheets.

| Sheet | Contents |
|---|---|
| Shipments | One row per shipment, with every count and percentage from the table. |
| Regions | One row per region of each shipment. |

The file uses the filters of the report on screen.
