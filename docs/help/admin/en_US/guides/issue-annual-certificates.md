# How to issue annual certificates

Once a year, each participant gets a certificate for each scheme they took part in. This guide issues them. Do it after every shipment of the year is finalized.

Go to **Reports → Annual Certificates**. The page shows one step at a time and opens the next step only when the current one is complete. If you leave, the page opens at the same step when you come back.

## Step 1: Choose the shipments

1. Pick the **Year**.
2. Untick any shipment the certificates must not cover.
3. Click **Continue**.

A participant gets a certificate for a scheme only if they responded to every ticked shipment of that scheme. If different labs took different shipments of one scheme, such as screening and confirmatory panels, tick one group now. Make a second batch for the other group when the first is done.

## Step 2: Check the templates

Each scheme needs an Excellence template and a Participation template, with the participant name placed on each.

- If every template shows a tick, click **Continue**.
- If a template is marked, click **Fix the templates**. Fix it in the new tab, then come back and click **Check again**.

[See: How to set up a certificate template](make-a-certificate-template.md)

## Step 3: Check who gets which certificate

1. Check the number of Excellence and Participation certificates for each scheme.
2. Open **Show the participants who get no certificate**, and check the reasons.
3. Click **Generate certificates**.

If a yellow warning says many labs missed a shipment, the ticked shipments probably went to different labs. Click **Change** in step 1 and tick one group.

The review spreadsheet is optional. It has every participant's results and certificate type.

## Step 4: Generate

Wait for generation to finish. It takes up to a few minutes. You can leave the page, and generation carries on.

If generation stops with an error, read the message, fix the cause and click **Start again**.

## Step 5: Review and approve

1. Click **View** on a few certificates to open them.
2. Check the **No certificate** tab.
3. Click **Approve and make available to participants**.

Participants see nothing before approval. If something is wrong, click **Discard this batch**, fix the cause and start again from step 1.

## Step 6: Check that it is done

Step 6 shows **Done** when each participant can download their certificate. Participants find it under **Downloads** in their account. ePT does not email them.

To issue certificates for another group of shipments, click **Start another batch**.

**Past batches**, at the top of the page, lists the batches of earlier years.
