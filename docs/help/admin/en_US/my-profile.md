# My Profile

This page holds your own admin account details. Every admin can open
it.

Click your first name at the top right of the page, then click
**My Profile**.

## Change your details

1. Under **Personal Information**, change the fields you need:

   | Field | Use |
   | --- | --- |
   | **First Name** | Your given name. Required |
   | **Last Name** | Your family name. Required |
   | **Primary Email** | The address you log in with |
   | **Secondary Email** | A second address |
   | **Phone** | Your phone number, with the country code |
   | **Language** | The language of the admin pages |

2. Click **Update Profile**.

The dashboard opens and shows *Profile updated successfully.*

If you change **Primary Email**, log in with the new address from
then on.

The admin pages switch to the new **Language** after you save.

## Change your password

1. Under **Change Password**, enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter the **New Password**. It needs at least 8 characters,
   with a letter, a number and a symbol.
3. Enter it again in **Confirm New Password**. The hint below the
   field shows whether the two match.
4. Click **Update Profile**.

If the current password is wrong, nothing is saved and the page
says so.

To keep your current password, leave the password fields empty.

To leave the page without saving, click **Back**.
