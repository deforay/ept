# Certificate Batches

This page lists every certificate batch, past and present. To
issue, review or approve certificates, use **Annual
Certificates**. See [Annual Certificates](certificates.md).

## Find a batch

Filter by **Status** or **Date Range**. Click **Reset** to clear
the filters.

Each row shows the **Batch Name**, the **Shipments** it covers,
and how many **Excellence**, **Participation** and **Skipped**
certificates it has.

## Batch statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** or **Generating** | The certificates are being made |
| **Generated** | Ready for review in Annual Certificates |
| **Approved** or **Distributing** | The certificates are being made available to participants |
| **Distributed** | Participants can download their certificates |
| **Failed** | Generation stopped with an error. Open it in Annual Certificates to see why |
| **Rejected** or **Cancelled** | The batch was discarded |

A batch in progress updates on the page by itself.

## Actions

- **Download ZIP** or **Download** saves the batch's certificates.
- **Open in Annual Certificates** opens the batch where you can
  review, approve or discard it.
