# Feedback Questions

This page is the question library. You pick questions from it
when you build a feedback form for a shipment. See
[Feedback Forms](feedback-forms.md).

## Add a question

1. Click **Add New Feed Back Question**.
2. Enter the **Question** text that participants see.
3. Enter a short **Question Code**, used in the downloaded
   responses.
4. Under **Show feedback question to**, choose **All
   Participants**, **Only Passing Participants** or **Only
   Failing Participants**.
5. Choose the **Question Type**: **Text**, **Numeric**, **Date
   Time** or **Drop down**. For **Drop down**, enter each choice in the **Options** rows.
6. Set **Question Status** to **Active**.
7. Click **Add**.

## Change or retire a question

Click **Edit** on the question's row. To stop offering a
question, set **Question Status** to **Inactive**.

An inactive question disappears from every form, including forms
that already use it. Its answers are also hidden. Do not
deactivate a question while a form that uses it is open for
feedback.
