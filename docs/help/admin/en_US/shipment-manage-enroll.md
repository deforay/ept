# Manage Enrollment

You are on this page because you clicked **Enrollment** on a
shipment that is already shipped. Before shipping, the shipment
row shows **Enroll** instead, which opens the participant picker.

The top of the page shows the shipment's code, scheme, PT Survey
and **Result Due Date**.

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Responded** | Enrolled participants who sent a response |
| **Not Responded** | Enrolled participants who have not responded yet |
| **Not Enrolled in this Shipment** | Active participants who are not on this shipment |

Click **Export to excel** to save the open tab as a file.

## Add a participant after shipping

1. Open the **Not Enrolled in this Shipment** tab.
2. Find the participant. Use the search boxes to narrow the list.
3. Click **Enroll** on the row. To add several, select them and
   click **Enroll** at the bottom.
4. Confirm. The participant can now see the shipment and respond.

## Remove a participant

Click **Delete** on the participant's row and confirm. This
cannot be undone. If the shipment was already evaluated,
evaluate it again so the counts are right.

## Copy email addresses

The **Copy Emails** buttons copy addresses to the clipboard, so
you can paste them into your own email:

- **Copy Emails : Enrolled** — participants and data managers on
  this shipment.
- **Copy Emails : Not Responded** — those who have not responded.
- **Copy Emails : Not Enrolled** — active participants not on
  this shipment.
- **Copy Emails : PTCC** — the country coordinators for the
  enrolled participants.

To send a reminder from ePT instead, use **Remind** on the
shipment row. See [Email Participants](email-participants.md).
