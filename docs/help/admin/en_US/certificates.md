# Annual Certificates

This page issues each participant's yearly certificate for each
scheme. Do it after every shipment of the year is finalized.

The page shows one step at a time. It opens the next step only
when the current one is complete. If you leave, the page opens at
the same step when you come back.

For the full walkthrough, see
[How to issue annual certificates](guides/issue-annual-certificates.md).

## The six steps

1. **Choose the shipments.** Pick the **Year** and untick any
   shipment the certificates must not cover.
2. **Check the templates.** Each scheme needs an Excellence and a
   Participation template, with the participant name placed.
3. **Check who gets which certificate.** Check the numbers, and
   the participants who get no certificate.
4. **Generate.** Wait a few minutes. You can leave the page.
5. **Review and approve.** Open a few certificates, then click
   **Approve and make available to participants**.
6. **Done.** Participants download their certificate under
   **Downloads** in their account. ePT does not email them.

## Who gets which certificate

- **Excellence**: passed every ticked shipment of the scheme.
- **Participation**: responded to every ticked shipment, but did
  not pass them all.
- **No certificate**: missed at least one ticked shipment.

## When something blocks you

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *No finalized shipments in this year* | Pick another year, or finalize the year's shipments first |
| A template is marked in step 2 | Click **Fix the templates**, fix it in the new tab, then click **Check again** |
| A yellow warning that many labs missed a shipment | The ticked shipments went to different labs. Click **Change** in step 1 and tick one group. Make a second batch for the other group |
| Generation stops with an error | Read the message, fix the cause and click **Start again** |
| A certificate looks wrong | Click **Discard this batch**, fix the cause and start again from step 1 |

Participants see nothing until you approve the batch.

**Past batches**, at the top of the page, lists the batches of
earlier years.
