# Shipments

A **shipment** is one set of test samples that you send out to
participants as part of a PT Survey. One PT Survey can hold one
or more shipments.

A shipment includes:

- A **scheme** (the test type, like HIV Serology or VL).
- A list of **samples** with their expected results.
- The **participants** you want to enroll for this shipment.

You always add shipments under a PT Survey. So the steps are:

1. First create the PT Survey (date and code).
2. Then add one or more shipments to it.
3. Then enroll participants in each shipment.
4. Then click **Ship Now** on the PT Survey page.

## What's on the page

This page shows all the shipments in your system. You can:

- **Search** by shipment code, scheme, or PT Survey.
- **Sort** by clicking the column headers.
- Use the buttons in each row. Which ones show depends on the
  shipment's status.

| Button | When it shows | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Until the shipment is finalized | Change samples, the due date and other details |
| **Clone** | Always | Starts a new shipment with the same samples and settings |
| **Enroll** | Before shipping | Choose the participants who get the shipment. Red means no participants yet |
| **Enrollment** | After shipping | See who responded, and add participants who are not enrolled |
| **Remind** | After shipping, while some labs have not responded | Opens Email Participants for this shipment |
| **Set Up Feedback Form** | The shipment collects feedback but has no form | Build the form. The shipment cannot be finalized without it |
| **Cancel** | Until the shipment is finalized | Cancels the shipment. You give a reason. This cannot be undone |

## Common things you do here

- **Add a new shipment** — pick the test type in **Scheme Type**
  at the top of this page, then click **New Shipment**. Or go to
  **Manage → PT Survey** and click **Add Shipment** next to your
  survey. That opens this page for that survey.
- **Add or remove participants** — find your shipment, click
  **Enroll**, click names to move them between the two sides,
  then click **Enroll** to save.
- **Edit samples or the result due date** — click **Edit**
  on the shipment, change the fields, then save.

## When a shipment becomes visible to participants

Shipments are **not** visible to participants right after you
create them. Participants only see a shipment after you click
**Ship Now** on its PT Survey. Until then, you can keep changing
samples, enrollments, and the due date safely.

## Tips

- A PT Survey with **no shipments** cannot be shipped. The
  **Ship Now** button will not appear.
- Participants enrolled in the **scheme** (Configure → Scheme
  Enrollments) are selected by default when you click **Enroll**.
  You can add other active participants on the same page.
- After you ship, avoid changing samples or enrollments.
  Participants may have already started entering results.
