# XTPT Indicators & Panel Statistics

**Reports → XTPT Indicators & Panel Statistics** builds an Excel workbook for one TB shipment.

## Generate a workbook

1. Pick the shipment in **Shipment**. The list holds every TB shipment that is not cancelled, newest first.
2. Click **Generate workbook**.
3. Click **Download** next to the workbook.

Building the workbook can take a minute. Each workbook you build stays in the **Workbooks** list until you leave the page.

## Workbook contents

| Sheet | Contents |
|---|---|
| Panel Statistics | The participating sites, responses received and excluded, participants able to submit, and participants scoring 80% or higher and 100%. Then the countries with non-participating sites, the error codes, the discordant results, and the countries and participants that reported discordant results. |
| All Sites' Results | One row per participant, with the results of every sample. |
